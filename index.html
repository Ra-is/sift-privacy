Sift

Find any screenshot. Everything stays on your iPhone.

A screenshot is an unfinished task, not a photograph — the price you meant to compare, the booking reference you needed later, the address someone sent you. Sift reads them all on your device and gives you them back when you need them.

How it works

  • Sift reads the text in your screenshots using your iPhone's own on-device intelligence.
  • It picks out what matters — prices, dates, phone numbers, reference codes.
  • Anything taken before you installed Sift is archived and searchable, so your inbox can reach zero on the first day.
  • No account, no servers, no internet connection required.
